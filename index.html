Welcome to my research website. I am a Senior Research Fellow at the Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD), where I oversee nanofabrication activities for Prof. Joel Yang's research group in the Engineering Product Development pillar.

With more than 10 years of experience, my work brings together thin-film optical coatings, photonic devices, advanced metrology, and CMOS-compatible micro- and nanofabrication. I translate research concepts through optical modelling, process development, device fabrication, experimental validation, and technology transfer.

My research spans structural color, multispectral filters, thermoelectric photodetectors, and single-photon-source technologies. I have authored or co-authored more than 20 peer-reviewed papers, with work appearing in Nanophotonics, Nano Letters, Advanced Optical Materials, Nature Communications, and Science Advances. I received my Ph.D. in Materials Science and Engineering from KAIST in 2017 and worked as a postdoctoral researcher at KIST before joining SUTD.

Research Interests

  • Thin films & optical coatings
  • Electron-beam lithography
  • Structural color & microcavities
  • Nanophotonics & metasurfaces
  • Optical & thermoelectric devices
  • Process development & integration
