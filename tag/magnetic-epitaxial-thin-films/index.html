On the growth and coalescence of the epitaxial islands of L10-FePt (001) thin-film

Journal of Applied Physics · 2016
DOI: 10.1063/1.4966169

Summary

High-resolution transmission electron microscopy reveals how epitaxial FePt islands grow into networks and eventually coalesce. The study identifies crack-closure and groove-filling mechanisms, and examines defects at the boundaries formed as islands merge. Coercivity and the magnetic nucleation field decrease as coalescence progresses, linking thin-film growth and interface structure to magnetic behavior.

Effect of Li substitution on the magnetic properties of LixMg0.40Ni0.60−2xFe2+xO4 ferrites

Physica B: Condensed Matter · 2011
DOI: 10.1016/j.physb.2011.01.058

Summary

Lithium substitution changes the structure and magnetic response of spinel ferrites. Lattice constants and grain size increase with lithium content, while initial permeability depends on composition and sintering temperature.
