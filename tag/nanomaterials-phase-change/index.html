Tunable Multiresonant Microcavity Exciton-Polaritons in Colloidal Quantum Wells

Nano Letters · 2025
DOI: 10.1021/acs.nanolett.5c00015

Summary

Continuously tunable optical states of highly textured Sb2Te3

Optical Materials Express · 2025
DOI: 10.1364/OME.564116

Summary

Gradual disordering of textured Sb2Te3 tunes its optical response between metallic-like and dielectric states. Resonant films and simulated reflectors demonstrate a route toward continuously adjustable reflective color.
