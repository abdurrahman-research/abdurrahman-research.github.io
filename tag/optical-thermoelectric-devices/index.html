Scaling up multispectral color filters with binary lithography and reflow (BLR)

Nanophotonics · 2024
DOI: 10.1515/nanoph-2024-0090

Summary

Binary lithography followed by polymer reflow creates at least 16 Fabry–Perot transmission filters in a single patterning step, offering approximately 15 nm control of dielectric thickness.

Sb2Te3–Bi2Te3 Direct Photo–Thermoelectric Mid-Infrared Detection

Advanced Optical Materials · 2024
DOI: 10.1002/adom.202401450

Summary

A resonant Sb2Te3–Bi2Te3 junction directly absorbs mid-infrared light and converts heating to an electrical signal. Devices operating at 3.6 μm achieved 10.2 V/W responsivity and bandwidth around 1 kHz.

Interband plasmonic nanoresonators for enhanced thermoelectric photodetection

Nanophotonics · 2025
DOI: 10.1515/nanoph-2024-0752

Summary

Nanoresonators patterned directly into Sb2Te3 and Bi2Te3 combine interband plasmonic absorption with thermoelectric conversion. The detector reaches approximately 90% visible absorption and a 160 μs response time.

Optical Wireless Power Transmission using Thermoelectric Device with Thin-Film Absorber

IEEE Journal on Wireless Power Technologies · 2026
DOI: 10.1109/JWPT.2026.3682232

Summary

An interference-based thin-film absorber converts laser light into heat for thermoelectric wireless power reception. A 450 nm, 1 W laser produced 2.0 mW electrical output; thermal spreading lets unilluminated elements also contribute.
